The generation findings below were the first pass; the board-generation work continued in `generators.md`, which validates these metrics against real play data and supersedes Part 1 where they disagree.

## Part 1 — Board generation: why boards feel stale/bland

### The short version

`generateWithWord` (Tue–Sun) has four structural biases that compound. None of them is a bug in isolation, but together they produce a recognisable "house style": an E-heavy, vowel-heavy board whose words cluster around one hub tile, with a lot of `see/tee/nee/eel/ere/too/moo` filler, anchored by a 9–11-letter seed word almost nobody will find. Mondays (`generateClassic`) have the opposite problem: no quality floor at all, and roughly one Monday in six is a 15–30-word dud.

### 1. The fill "scoring" is a no-op except for one thing: it rewards EE and OO

`generateBoard.ts` L140-155 `scoreCandidate(candidate, letter)` returns `doubleBonus - averageDistance`. For the *fill* phase (L195-199) the cell is fixed and only the letter varies, and `averageDistance` depends only on the cell and whether the letter is a vowel — so every consonant gets the same score, and every vowel gets the same score, **except `e` and `o`, which get +2 if the same letter is already adjacent**. Because +2 dwarfs the distance term (range ≈ 0 to −3), whenever an `e` or `o` neighbours an empty cell that was randomly assigned "vowel", the fill will place a second `e`/`o` there every time the no-triple rule allows it.

Measured effect (synthetic 4×4, N=1500):

| | |
|---|---|
| boards with an adjacent E–E pair | **57.3%** |
| boards with an adjacent O–O pair | **39.1%** |
| boards with ≥1 EE/OO pair | **77.6%** |
| share of all findable words containing "ee" or "oo" | 9.5% (dictionary baseline 5.1%) |
| `e` share of all board tiles | **12.7%** (classic dice: 10.0%; English text ≈ 12.7%, but English text is not a word list) |

Consequence: the most recurring words across production boards are `see` (46% of recent 4×4 boards), `tee`, `seer`, `ere`, `lee`, `eel`, `pee`, `nee`, `peer`, `too`, `moo`. Across synthetic boards `see`/`tee` appear on 28% of boards vs `tea`/`ate` at 13% for the classic dice. This is the single most identifiable source of "I've seen this board before".

Also note the vowel fill at L195 draws from `"aeiou".split("")` — **uniform**, not from `vowelPool` — so `u` and `i` are as likely as `e` and `a` in filled cells, which is why awkward `u`-heavy boards (`reuhraldtnreeuaiufctahlur`, Sep 19; `uhsreeiuhsbbulaq`, Sep 23) appear.

### 2. The seed word is chosen uniformly from 23,831 obscure words, and it is the strongest predictor of board quality

L101-104: `pick(rng, list.filter(len 9–11))`. That pool is 33% of the whole dictionary and is dominated by inflected and rare forms:

| form | share of pool |
|---|---|
| base word | 36.7% |
| plural (-s/-es) | 24.5% |
| -ing | 12.4% |
| -ed | 10.2% |
| -ly / -er / -ers / -est / -ness / -ies | 16.2% |

So ~63% of days are anchored by an inflection, and the base words are mostly things like `clerestory`, `garrulous`, `heterodox`, `bivouacked`, `mortising`, `intuiting`, `aerodrome`, `exhumation` (all real production seeds from the last six weeks). The seed word occupies 56–69% of a 4×4 grid, so it *is* the board.

Correlation with total word count (synthetic 4×4, N=2000):

| feature | Pearson r |
|---|---|
| **mean dictionary-frequency of the seed word's letters** | **+0.40** |
| count of j/q/x/z/v/w/k on board | −0.18 |
| distinct letters | +0.10 |
| vowel count | −0.07 (non-linear, see §3) |
| EE/OO pairs | +0.02 |

Nothing else comes close. The generator spends most of the board on a word chosen without any regard to how productive its letters are, and the richness of the day is essentially a lottery on that pick. Production examples: `bivouacked` → 54 words, `garrulous` → 60, `exceptional` → 65, `aerodrome` → 71; versus `displeased` → 246, `hesitated` → 303, `interpolate` → 350. A 5× spread between consecutive weekdays reads as "some days are bland".

~0.9% of the seed pool can never be placed at all (`accelerate`, `bookkeeper`, `continuum`, `aardvarks`…) because `createsBadDouble` (L123-124) forbids adjacent identical `a/u/i/c/h/k/…`, and the DFS silently fails. The board is then a pure random fill with no seed word and nothing tells you.

### 3. Vowel ratio is too high and too variable

Classic Boggle dice yield ≈38% vowels. `generateWithWord` fills with `rng() < 0.46` (L195) on top of whatever the seed word brings, giving a mean of 41% (4×4) / 43% (5×5) and a wide spread — production boards hit 56% (`automobiles`, `aerodrome`, `nauseates`). The bucketed effect on word count is non-linear:

| vowels on 4×4 board | share of boards | mean words |
|---|---|---|
| 4 | 6.7% | 94 |
| 5–7 | 66% | 108–110 |
| 8 | 15.8% | 103 |
| 9 | 7.2% | 93 |
| 10 | 2.2% | 84 |

About a quarter of boards sit in the ≥8-vowel tail where words get shorter and "vowelly" (`eau`, `aero`, `oleo`, `ion`, `moi`, `roo`), which is a large part of what "bland" feels like. The 10-vowel floor of 84 is not catastrophic for the *count*, but the length distribution shifts hard toward 3-letter words (30.6% of words are 3 letters on word boards; 44.5% on classic).

### 4. Everything funnels through one hub tile

Mean share of a board's words that pass through the single most-used tile: **64.7%**. The seed-word path is laid down first with a distance-to-opposite-type preference (L146-150) that pulls vowels and consonants into a compact blob; then the fill prioritises cells with the most filled neighbours (L192). The result is a dense productive core (usually around the `e`/`s`/`t`/`r` of the seed word) and a sparse rim. Weak tiles (used by <5% of words) average 0.9 per 4×4 and 3.5 per 5×5 board. Players feel this as "the board is really just these six tiles".

### 5. Letter diversity is lower than classic, and seed-word repeats make it worse

Distinct letters per 4×4: word boards median 11 (min 6), classic median 12 (min 7). Seeds with internal repeats (`commentates`, `smokiness`, `persimmon`, `nauseates`, `aerodrome` → 8 distinct letters on the board) leave little room for anything else. Boards with 7–8 distinct letters average 71–87 words vs 105–111 at 11–12.

### 6. Dead-letter repair injects random junk

`removeDeadLetters` (L205-222) replaces unused tiles with `pick(rng, alphabet)` — uniform over 26 letters — so 15% of repairs are `j/q/x/z`. It never fails (0% of boards keep dead letters), but it's why an `x` or `q` turns up on an otherwise unrelated board (`heterodox`'s board also got a second `x`; `rsjdbrseaoadtalk`; `uhsreeiuhsbbulaq`). These are the tiles players complain about.

### 7. Mondays (`generateClassic`) have no floor

| classic 4×4 | share |
|---|---|
| < 30 words | 16.2% |
| < 40 words | 29.8% |
| < 50 words | 45.8% |

Production: Sep 21 → **15 words**, Oct 19 → **20 words**, Oct 5 → 52, Sep 14 → 47. Classic also places `q` without the `u` rule (`nuulntlqemttemhx`). The old client-side "reroll if < 130 words" rule (see `scripts/boards/exportBoards.ts` header) was removed when boards moved to the DB; nothing replaced it. 93 of 158 historical boards were rerolls under that rule, i.e. the *old* generator was failing its own bar 60% of the time, and the current one would fail a 130-word bar 77% of the time on 4×4.

### 8. Smaller things

- `generateWithWord` runs `solve()` up to 21 times (L209) and `list.filter` over 72k words on every call; fine for a daily cron, but `resolvePracticeBoard` runs it per practice request.
- `rankedBy` (L157-158) shuffles then uses `Array.sort` with a comparator — stable in modern V8, so tie order follows the shuffle; OK, but it means the "ranking" is a weighted-random draw wearing a scoring costume. Worth making explicit when redesigning.
- `createsBadDouble` forbids `hh`/`kk`/`cc`/`vv`/`ww`/`yy`/`xx` but not `ss`/`tt`/`ll`/`nn`/`mm`/`pp`/`rr`/`dd`/`ff`/`gg`/`bb`/`zz`; the triple rule only limits runs of 3. `tt`, `ss`, `ll` doubles are common and fine; `bb` (`rubbishes` board) and `zz` are not.
- Seed is the date string; `generateClassic`'s weekday mapping and the "0.46" vowelness are magic numbers with no provenance or test.

### What this means for the "second algorithm on certain days" ask

The data says the levers that matter are (a) which seed word you choose, (b) capping vowels around 35–40%, (c) killing the EE/OO bonus, (d) a hard quality floor with a reroll loop, and (e) measuring spread (distinct letters, tile-use concentration, 6+ letter word count) rather than raw word count. I have a design in mind but will hold it until you ask, per your instruction.

## Part 2 — Server, API & infrastructure

Severity: **H**igh / **M**edium / **L**ow.

### Security

- **[H] Tomorrow's board can be obtained via the challenge endpoint.** `netlify/functions/logPlayerWords.mts` L17-45 accepts any `dateKey` and any `playerId` with zero validation. `getChallenge.mts` L24-55 decodes an *unsigned* token (any date up to 65535 days after 2026-01-01), finds a `playerWords` row for `(date, playerId)` — which the attacker just inserted — then calls `getOrCreateBoard(dateKey)`, which generates, **persists**, and returns the future board with `totalWords`. This bypasses the future-date guard in `getBoard.mts` L19-24. Fix: validate `dateKey <= getGameDateKey()` in `logPlayerWords`, and refuse future dates in `getChallenge` (also consider signing challenge tokens with `SESSION_SECRET`).
- **[H] The Patreon archive paywall is bypassable.** `/api/board?dateKey=<any past date>` (`getBoard.mts`) returns full letters + `totalWords` for any date with no auth; `/api/player-words?dateKey=` returns Ava's words for any date. The SvelteKit routes are gated in `hooks.server.ts`, but the Netlify functions don't go through `handle`. Anyone can reconstruct the entire archive.
- **[H] Score and stats are fully client-trusted.** `logPlayerWords.mts` stores the client-sent `score`, `fairFight`, `playerId`, `challengedBy`, `words` as-is. `words` is never checked against `boards.totalWords`; `score` is never recomputed; no size cap on `words` or string length on `playerId`/`challengedBy` (`varchar` with no length → unbounded). Unlimited inserts per IP/player (no rate limit, no uniqueness on `(dateKey, playerId)`). The stats page, averages, top player, and "players" count are all poisonable with a single `curl`. Non-numeric or huge `score` → Postgres integer error → 500.
- **[M] Patreon access + refresh tokens live in the cookie.** `session.ts` L23-28: HMAC-signed but plaintext base64url. HttpOnly mitigates XSS, but logs/proxies/backups see live OAuth tokens. Prefer server-side session storage or encrypt the payload. There's also no `exp` in the payload; a stolen cookie is valid for 30 days and its `tier: "paid"` is trusted for 24h windows.
- **[M] Admin auth model.** `login.mts` returns the real long-lived `ADMIN_TOKEN` to the browser, which stores it in `localStorage["admin_token"]` and sends it as a bare `authorization` header; no rate limiting on the password endpoint; comparison uses `===` elsewhere (`getBoard`, `logAvasWords`, `rerollBoard`). `login.mts` L19-22 pads with spaces before `timingSafeEqual`, so `"secret   "` matches `"secret"`. Suggest: hash/HMAC the password server-side, issue a short-lived signed admin cookie, and `timingSafeEqual` on hashes.
- **[M] PII logged.** `patreon.ts` L123 `console.log(data, included)` dumps the full Patreon identity (name, email if scoped, membership) into Netlify function logs on every identity check.
- **[M] Stale slur list still shipped.** Commit `7da2f1e` "removed dud words" edited `static/wordList.txt` only. `src/lib/dictionary/wordList.txt` still contains `retard`, `retarded`, `gyp`, `gypped`, etc. It is imported by `Checker.svelte`, `checker/[word]/+page.ts` and `server/sitemap.ts`, so `/checker/retard` reports "valid word" and is **in the sitemap submitted to search engines**. Delete the `src/lib` copies and read from `static/` (or vice-versa) so there's one source of truth.
- **[L] `oauth_state` cookie** (`auth/login/+server.ts`) lacks `secure`. `auth/callback` doesn't wrap `fetchIdentity` in try/catch → raw 500.
- **[L] `crypto` npm package** (`package.json`) is the deprecated placeholder; `login.mts` should import `node:crypto`. `netlify-cli` (≈ 300 MB) is in `dependencies` rather than `devDependencies`. `@neondatabase/serverless` is imported directly but only present transitively via `@netlify/neon`. `@resvg/resvg-js` is unused.

### Correctness / technical faults

- **[M] `/play` can be cached for a day without Ava's words.** `play/+page.server.ts` L12-16 embeds `avasWords` into a page cached with `s-maxage=86400`. `rebuild.mts` purges at 14:00 UTC *after* creating the board, but if Ava's words land after the first request of the day, every player sees `avasWords: null` until the next purge. Combined with the client `hasComparison` bug (Part 3), their stored scores will be inflated by +1/word. Either purge `play-page` in `logAvasWords`, or fetch Ava's words client-side at reveal.
- **[M] SSR fetches its own API over HTTP.** `play/+page.server.ts` L20-24 does `globalThis.fetch(url.origin + "/api/board")` from inside the SSR function to reach a Netlify function. That's an extra public round-trip, depends on `url.origin` being routable (deploy previews / branch deploys / `netlify dev`), and returns 503 on any hiccup. `getOrCreateBoard` is importable and used by the function anyway; call it directly.
- **[M] `/api/player-words` GET throws for days without Ava's words.** `logPlayerWords.mts` L75-82: `ava.words` on `undefined` → TypeError → 500.
- **[M] Archive & challenge plays pollute daily stats.** `archive/[date]/+page.svelte` and `challenge/+page.svelte` both render `<Game playerStatus="player">`, so finishing an archived board inserts a `playerWords` row for that old date; `getDayWordStats` (`words.ts`) counts every row as a player, including repeat plays of the same `playerId`, challenge rematches, and extra-time games. Add a `mode` column (`daily | archive | challenge`) or filter on `challengedBy IS NULL AND created_at within the game day`.
- **[M] `getScores`/`getWordPlayPercent` use durable CDN cache + `stats-page` tag** but the `/stats/[date]` page (not cached) and archive plays mean the same data has two freshness regimes. Minor inconsistency; document which one is canonical.
- **[L] `archive/+page.server.ts`** recomputes "today" with a hard-coded `10 * HOUR` instead of `getGameDateKey()`; the module-level date-key cache is per lambda instance, so different instances disagree for up to an hour. `archive/[date]/+page.server.ts` `cachedGames` is unbounded and never invalidated (a reroll or re-logged Ava words won't show until the instance recycles).
- **[L] Game-day rollover ignores DST.** `getGameDateKey` uses fixed UTC+10; `rebuild.mts` is `0 14 * * *` (= 00:00 AEST, 01:00 AEDT). Consistent with each other, but Sydney players get the new board at 1am in summer. Probably intentional — worth a one-line note in `gameDate.ts`.
- **[L] `rerollBoard`** upserts `letters`/`totalWords` only (fine, same weekday) and deletes Ava's words, but doesn't purge the `play-page` CDN tag — only matters if a same-day reroll is ever allowed.
- **[L] `dictionaryManager`** is a mutable module singleton shared by the server (`loadDictionaryFromDisk`) and the client (`load.ts`). `DictionaryManager.init` doesn't lowercase or strip non-`a-z`; `Trie.insert` would index `charCodeAt - 97` out of range on any stray character (the current list is clean, but there's no guard).
- **[L] `solver.ts`** rebuilds a 2-D grid and uses recursion with 8 neighbour checks and bounds checks per step; fine at this size, but `GameManager.findPath` and `generationAnalysis` reimplement the same DFS. One shared `forEachPath` would remove three copies.

### Maintainability / repo hygiene

- Stray artefacts at root: `board_difficulty.csv`, `board_difficulty copy.csv`, `comparison.png`, `src/routes/Artboard 3.svg`, `static/Asset {12,22,32,42}.svg`, `openapi.json` (should live next to `wiktionary/types.ts`), `svelte-sitemap.config.ts` (package not installed; sitemap is hand-rolled), `deno.lock` alongside `bun.lockb`.
- `package.json` `"difficulty"` script points at `src/lib/scripts/boardDifficulty.ts`, which doesn't exist. `scripts/boards/test.ts` imports `WEEKDAYS` from `boardSettings` (it's in `board.ts`) and no longer compiles. `exportBoards.ts` says "run once … then this file can go".
- `migrations/0005_seed_boards.sql` is a 138 KB data migration run by `netlify.toml`'s build command on every deploy (`bun run db:migrate && bun run build`). Drizzle skips applied migrations, but a failed/half-applied state on a preview DB will block builds; consider separating schema migrations from data seeding.
- `netlify.toml` `included_files` ships `static/wordList.txt`/`dirtyWords.txt` into every function bundle, and `loadDictionaryFromDisk` reads it with `process.cwd()`; it works on Netlify but is implicit. Importing with `?raw` (as `sitemap.ts` does) would make the dependency explicit and bundle-safe.
- Two prettier styles coexist (`.prettierrc` says 4-space/no-semi; `Reveal.svelte`, `Toast.svelte`, `Board.svelte`, `login.mts` use semicolons; several files use tabs). Running `prettier --write` once would stop the churn.
- No tests of any kind. The scoring function, date rollover, challenge token codec, trie/solver, and generator are all pure and trivially testable; `vite.config.ts` even has a `VITEST` branch.
- `README.md` TODO is empty; there's no documentation of the env vars (`ADMIN_TOKEN`, `ADMIN_PASSWORD`, `SESSION_SECRET`, `PATREON_*`, `NETLIFY_DATABASE_URL*`).

## Part 3 — Client (delegated review, verified spot-checks)

Full detail is in the sub-agent report captured in the conversation; the items below were independently confirmed by reading the code.

- **[H] Intro `setTimeout` is never cleared** — `Game.svelte` L73-76. Leaving `/play` during the 3.2 s intro lets `startGame()` fire on a destroyed component; the interval ticks down `secondsLeft` and writes localStorage every second, then `endGame()` POSTs. Come back later and your daily game has burned down. `onDestroy` calls `stopTimer()` but `#timerHandle` doesn't exist yet at that point.
- **[H] Keyboard trap** — `InputController.svelte.ts` L50-52 `preventDefault()`s *every* key while playing (Tab, Space, Escape, Cmd/Ctrl combos). Pause button can't be reached or activated by keyboard.
- **[H] Stored score ≠ displayed score when Ava hasn't played** — `GameManager.endGame` L132-137 omits `hasComparison`, so every word gets +1 "opponent bonus" in the DB while the UI shows the lower number. Same in `Stats.svelte` and `StreakManager`.
- **[H] 2.4 MB of dictionaries in the client bundle** — `Checker.svelte` L3-4 and `checker/[word]/+page.ts` L2-3 `?raw`-import `wordList.txt` + `scrabble.txt`; a 250k-entry `Set` is rebuilt per SSR request and per client load.
- **[M] `Dates.svelte` groups by month name only** (L29-38) — January 2027 merges into January 2026's calendar; breaks in ~10 weeks.
- **[M] `GameManager` constructor calls `load()` before `scoreTracker.init()`** — restored words are scored against the previous game's opponent.
- **[M] Timer runs while tab is hidden during the intro** (`suspend()` is a no-op unless `playing`).
- **[M] `challengeManager.showChallenge` singleton never resets** — once opened, the challenge panel appears on every subsequent game-over in the session.
- **[M] Unguarded `JSON.parse` of localStorage** in `session/gameState.ts` L23 — a corrupted entry kills the page.
- **[M] No error path on score submission** (`endGame` has no try/catch; `Reveal` spins forever; `if (data?.average)` drops a legitimate 0).
- **[M] `Iterator.prototype.toArray` in the typing hot path** (`GameManager.getSearchArea` L290) — throws on Safari < 18.4 / Firefox < 131; keyboard input is dead on those.
- **[M] Typing a word containing "qu" doesn't work** — chain compares raw tile letters; players must type `qit` for `quit`.
- **[M] Accessibility** — toasts have no `aria-live`; `maximum-scale=1.0, user-scalable=no` in `app.html`; toggles have no visible focus; board SVG has no role/label; dark-mode flash on load.
- **[M] Game-design** — "extra time" (+2 min) games are averaged and ranked with 3-minute games; zero-word games aren't recorded (biases averages upward); challenge games share the daily save slot `boggle_<dateKey>`; the whole answer key is in the HTML before play starts.
- **[L]** Dead code: `StreakManager.svelte.ts` (219 lines, no callers), `Streak.svelte` (empty), `ScoreTracker.pointsMap/totalWords/totalPossibleScore` (written, never read), `GameManager.isTentative`, `InputController.isSliding`, unused imports in `Reveal`, `Checker`, `Challenge`, `ScoreTracker`. Typos: `definitionManaager`, `defintion`. Default alphabet strings in `solver/+page.svelte` (missing `p`) and `PracticeForm.svelte` (missing `z`). `Tile.svelte` has a commented-out attribute inside a tag (`// font-weight="bold"`) and `transform: fill;`.
